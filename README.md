# The World Today — 21 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image,
for the make.fun `news-image-20261121` competition.

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only, no network) draws the page from scratch — its
own rasterizer, 5×7 bitmap type, and PNG encoder — and writes the single
1024×1024 image to `/output/entry.png`.

Lead story: a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile lands in Poland during a mass
attack on Ukraine. Also covered: the UK–EU post-Brexit deal, the ICJ
genocide ruling against Myanmar, the Wall Street AI sell-off, Trump
deferring an Iran decision, COP31 missing its deadline, and six stories
in brief.

Run: `node main.ts` (or `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` locally).
